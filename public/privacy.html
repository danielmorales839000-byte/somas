What you share

The feedback form collects your email, optional name, selected interest, message, and consent. Email addresses are self-reported and are not verified. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Why we collect it

The SOMAS founder uses submissions to understand interest in the concept and respond to your inquiry. Form submission does not subscribe you to a newsletter. We do not sell these submissions.

Storage and protection

Submissions are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and are accessible through a protected owner inbox. A daily rotating hash of your IP address is kept temporarily for rate limiting; the form does not store the raw address. Cloudflare may process technical request data to operate and protect the service. We aim to remove inquiries after 12 months unless an ongoing conversation requires keeping them longer.
